AYAT & ANIMALS

Look closer at the creatures Allah mentions, and be amazed.

Ayat & Animals makes short reels for families about the animals and insects mentioned in the Holy Quran and in authentic hadith. Each episode looks at how one creature was made, then ends with the verse, recited by a real reciter.

Our promise

  • Religious sources: only the Quran, Sahih al-Bukhari and Sahih Muslim, always with references.
  • Science facts: only from universities, museums, encyclopedias and peer-reviewed research.
  • No music. No people shown. Visuals are AI-generated.

About our publishing app

We use a small private app, "Ayat and Animals Uploader", only to upload our own videos to our own YouTube, TikTok, Instagram and Facebook accounts and to read our own posts' statistics. It is not offered to the public and does not access anyone else's accounts. See the Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.
