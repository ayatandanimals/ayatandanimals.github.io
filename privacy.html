PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated: 23 November 2026

This policy covers the Ayat & Animals website and our private publishing app, "Ayat and Animals Uploader" (the "App"). The App is used only by the Ayat & Animals studio to publish its own videos to its own accounts. It is not offered to the public.

What the App accesses

  • YouTube (YouTube API Services): uploading videos, setting titles, descriptions, thumbnails and captions, and reading view counts and comments on our own channel.
  • TikTok (Content Posting API): uploading videos to our own TikTok account and reading basic profile and video statistics.
  • Instagram and Facebook (Meta Graph API): publishing Reels and videos to our own Instagram account and Facebook Page and reading their insights and comments.

Personal data

The App only signs in to accounts owned by the studio. It does not collect, sell or share personal data about viewers. Public comments on our posts may be read so we can reply to them and keep our pages respectful. They are not stored beyond what is needed for that, and they are never sold or shared with third parties.

Access tokens are stored privately on the studio's own computer and are never shared. The website itself uses no cookies, analytics or trackers.

Google and YouTube

The App uses YouTube API Services. By using it you are also bound by the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. You can revoke the App's access at any time from Google security settings.

The App's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Data deletion

To remove any data the App holds, revoke the App's access in your Google, TikTok, Instagram or Facebook settings, and email ayatandanimals@gmail.com with the subject "Data deletion". We delete it within 30 days and confirm by email.

Children

Our videos are made for families. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes and contact

We will post any changes to this page. Questions: ayatandanimals@gmail.com.
